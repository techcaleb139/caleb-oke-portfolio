# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The current portfolio is a React 19 and Vite site deployed on Vercel. The redesign must preserve the existing working stack and integrations unless Caleb explicitly approves a technical migration.

## Users

The primary visitors are small business owners, online operators, creators, and hiring decision makers in Nigeria and international markets who may need practical automation work. They may be evaluating Caleb quickly from a phone or laptop and need to understand what he can build, what has actually been tested, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio helps visitors understand Caleb Oke's practical AI automation capabilities, evaluate evidence from his current projects, and submit a useful project brief. Success means a qualified visitor can understand his work and reach the brief form without encountering inflated claims or unclear technical language.

## Positioning

Caleb presents automation as accountable engineering: clear rules, visible failures, tested behavior, known limits, and human control. The portfolio must distinguish this from generic AI agency promises and one-click automation marketing.

## Operating Context

Visitors review the site on mobile and desktop. They evaluate workflow automation, data and API pipelines, and voice AI prototypes. The main proof comes from system diagrams, real workflow screenshots, test observations, known limitations, and direct links to code or demonstrations where available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current service areas: workflow automation, data and API pipelines, and voice AI prototypes.
- Current tools include n8n, Vapi, Make.com, Zapier, Python, JavaScript, Docker, and REST APIs.
- The contact brief is backed by Neon and must remain compatible with the existing automation.
- The redesign must preserve factual project status and cannot imply live client deployment where none exists.
- The Voice AI Restaurant Ordering work is a TS Academy final project built around a fictional Nigerian restaurant scenario.
- The Job Search Pipeline is a working personal system currently limited by local hosting.
- Existing routes, anchors, contact paths, SEO metadata, form field contracts, and deployment behavior must not change silently.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Caleb Oke.
- Role: AI Automation Builder and Computer Science student.
- Core philosophy: "I build practical automations for real work. Voice, workflow, and data systems built with clear rules, visible failures, and human control."
- Use the approved studio portrait of Caleb in a navy shirt against a grey background without altering his identity.
- The visual language must communicate trust, engineering rigor, transparency, and competence.
- Avoid flashy tech-bro styling and generic AI agency aesthetics.

## Evidence on Hand

- Voice AI Restaurant Ordering Prototype: a graded TS Academy prototype demonstrated end to end with test calls and sample data. It has not been validated in live restaurant operations.
- Automated Job Search Engine and Alert Pipeline: a working personal system that processed 110 listings and produced 3 high-priority alerts in the documented run. It currently depends on local hosting.
- Existing GitHub repositories, project screenshots, workflow canvases, and the approved portrait can be used as proof assets.
- There are no confirmed client testimonials, customer logos, revenue claims, or operational savings that may be invented for the page.

## Product Principles

1. Show evidence before making claims.
2. State known limits as clearly as tested results.
3. Translate technical systems into outcomes a business visitor can understand.
4. Keep humans in control of important decisions.
5. Make the next step clear without using pressure or hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must meet WCAG AA contrast, support keyboard navigation, preserve visible focus states, respect reduced motion, use readable body text, and work without hover on touch devices.
